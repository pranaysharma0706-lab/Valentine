Dear Sunidhi,

You know what? You are special to me. Not because I want to impress you or get something from you in any form, but simply because you matter to me.

I know we have had our fights, our arguments, our misunderstandings and moments when we both became angry. But despite all of that, one thing has always remained the same — the respect and love I have for you.

I respect you a lot. I never wanted our relationship to be about physical needs or anything close to that. For me, love is about understanding, caring, respecting and standing beside the person who truly matters.

And honestly... you are the one I want. ❤️

I love you because you were the first person to come into my life and introduce me to what love really feels like.

You showed me how beautiful it can be to care for someone, to miss someone, to worry about someone and to simply want that person to be okay.

Yesterday, when you were angry and said that I am a burden to my parents, I know there was anger behind those words. Maybe you were right about some things, and I understand that.

I know I still have a lot to improve in my life. I am trying. I want to become better, stronger and more responsible — not just for myself, but also for the people I love.

And yes, Pranika is my friend. Just a good friend. I really want you to understand that there is nothing more behind it.

Please don't let misunderstandings become bigger than the love and friendship we share.

♡ ✦ ♥ ✦ ♡
I am sorry. ❤️

Please understand me.
Please forgive me.
And whenever you feel ready...

Love you a lot,
Pranay ❤️
